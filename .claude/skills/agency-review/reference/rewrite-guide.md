# Copy & Conversion Rewrite Guide

Use this during the Copywriting and CRO passes. The goal: replace generic,
forgettable copy with specific, persuasive, conversion-focused language.

## Headlines
- Lead with the outcome the customer wants, not what you do.
  - Weak: "Welcome to our company"
  - Strong: "Sell your property in 21 days — guaranteed offer, no fees"
- Be specific. Numbers, timeframes, and concrete benefits beat adjectives.
- One idea per headline. Support it with a one-line subhead that adds proof or
  detail.

## Calls to Action
- Action + value, first person where it fits.
  - Weak: "Submit" / "Learn more"
  - Strong: "Get my free valuation" / "Book my 15-min call"
- Reduce risk next to the button: "No obligation · 60-second form."
- One primary CTA per view; secondary actions visually subordinate.

## Persuasion & Trust (CRO)
- **Social proof:** testimonials with names/photos, client logos, star ratings,
  counts ("Trusted by 1,200+ homeowners").
- **Authority:** credentials, awards, press mentions, guarantees.
- **Urgency/scarcity — only if true:** limited slots, deadlines, seasonal offers.
- **Risk reversal:** guarantees, free trials, "cancel anytime," money-back.
- **Objection handling:** a short FAQ that answers the real hesitations.

## Structure of a high-converting page
1. Hero: outcome headline + subhead + primary CTA + one trust signal.
2. Problem/agitation: name the pain in the visitor's words.
3. Solution: how you solve it, framed as benefits (not features).
4. Proof: testimonials, results, logos, data.
5. Offer: what they get, clearly; pricing or "how it works" steps.
6. Objections: FAQ / guarantee.
7. Final CTA: restate the offer and the primary action.

## Tone & clarity
- Write to one person, in plain language. Cut jargon and filler.
- Short sentences. Active voice. Concrete nouns.
- Read every line and ask: "So what?" If there's no clear answer, cut or rewrite.

## Micro-copy that lifts conversion
- Form field helper text and inline validation messages.
- Button-adjacent reassurance ("We'll never share your email").
- Empty states, success messages, and error messages written like a helpful human.
