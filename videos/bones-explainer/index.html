Your skeleton. Protecting you, every single day.
Right now, 206 bones are holding you together.
And most of the time, you never even notice them.
But bones aren't dry, dead sticks. They're living tissue.
A hard outer shell of compact bone. A light honeycomb of spongy bone inside.
And at the core, marrow, making billions of new blood cells every day.
That design makes bone light, but incredibly tough. It takes several times your body weight with every running stride.
Bones are also your built-in armour.
Your skull encases your brain.
Your ribcage shields your heart and lungs.
And your spine guards the spinal cord, your body's main information highway.
Even when a bone breaks, it rebuilds itself, bridging the gap with brand new bone.
