    Assumptions in this sample model
    • Ideal yield 950 kWh per kWp per year (south, 35°, no shade).
    • Direction and pitch scale that yield. South at 35° is 100%; north-facing is 57%.
    • Monthly shape is a typical UK curve, June and July strongest.
    • Household use is bill divided by unit price, spread flat across the year.
    • Exported power earns 15 p per kWh.
    • Cost is £1,650 per kWp, plus £3,800 for a battery. Output falls 0.5% a year.
    • Every figure is a placeholder for the concept, not a quotation.
