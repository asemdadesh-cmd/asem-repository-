# Launch kit: selling the websites

Practical, copy-paste. Everything here is a starting point, not a promise of results. Prices, names and turnaround are placeholders: set yours.

## 1. The offer in one line

> **Scroll-driven websites for small businesses. Built for phones first, in English and Arabic. You own the files.**

Why it sells: most local sites are static templates. Yours move when the visitor does, and you can *show* that in ten seconds on a phone. The five demos in `web/demos/` are your proof. The clips in `web/assets/work/*.mp4` are ready-made content.

## 2. Who to chase first (pick ONE niche for the first 30 days)

Recommendation: **estate agents and lettings agents.** You already live in that world (NHT Estates), you know what a good listing looks like, and the Haldane & Rowe demo speaks their language. A niche beats "any business" because your outreach, demo and case study all reuse.

Second wave, easiest to approach by walking in: restaurants/cafés, gyms, barbers and salons, solar/trade installers.

Skip for now: shops that need a full e-commerce build, anyone who wants a £150 site.

## 3. Pricing (placeholders already on the site)

| Package | On the site | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| One page | from £490 | The volume product. Keep scope tight: one page, one form. |
| Signature | from £1,290 | Up to five pages + one custom interaction (calculator, menu, booking nudge). Bilingual EN/AR sits here. |
| Custom | Ask | Quote after a call. |

Rules that protect you: 50% deposit to start, balance before launch; two rounds of changes; extra rounds charged hourly. Hosting on the client's own Netlify/Vercel/Cloudflare free tier, so you carry no monthly bill and they are not locked in. Offer an optional monthly care plan later (updates, backups), not on day one.

## 7. The "free preview" play (use sparingly)

For your first 5 prospects only, build a 20-second clip of their *own* hero (their name, colours, logo) using the closest demo as the base. It costs you an hour, it converts far better than a cold pitch, and it becomes your first case study if they say yes. Cap it: five previews, then charge for the preview (deducted from the deposit).

## 8. First 10 clients, a realistic loop

1. Week 1: pick the niche, set up Facebook + Instagram, publish the site, post 4 reels.
2. Weeks 2 to 4: 15 personalised messages a day for 5 days a week; track replies in a sheet (name, date, reply, next step).
3. Every call: one question first, "What's the best enquiry you ever got from your website?" Their answer is your pitch.
4. After the first sale: ask for a short written testimonial and permission to show the site. Only then add it to the page; never invent one.
5. Keep a "no thanks" list; revisit in 90 days.

## 9. Housekeeping before you take money (UK)

Check these yourself or with an adviser; this is not legal advice.

- A one-page contract: scope, price, deposit, revision rounds, ownership transfer on final payment, who supplies images and text.
- Data protection: if client sites collect enquiries, they may need a privacy notice, and you may need to pay the ICO data protection fee.
- Images and fonts: the demos use only original SVG art and OFL fonts (see `web/assets/fonts/`). For clients, use their own photos or licensed ones.
- Do not copy another business's name, logo or text into a demo.

## 10. Customising a demo for a client

See `web/README.md` section "Making a client site from a demo". Rule of thumb: Haldane & Rowe for property, Altura for product stories, Iron Round for energy brands, Tannour for anything bilingual or type-led, Sundial for businesses that quote or calculate.
