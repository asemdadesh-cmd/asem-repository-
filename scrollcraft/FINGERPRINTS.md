# Fingerprints

Registry for the Scroll Craft fingerprint gate (skill: `nateherkai/scroll-craft`, `references/uniqueness.md`). A new build must differ from every row on at least 4 of 6 dimensions. The first row is copied from `asemdadesh-cmd/wasma-next/scrollcraft/FINGERPRINTS.md` so builds here are gated against it too.

| Build | Grammar | Nav treatment | Hero device | Act-sequence shape | Close pattern | Signature move | World | Port |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| wasma-next | Gallery with editorial interludes | Fixed paper bar, wordmark, four anchors, language switch, one CTA; full-screen modal menu on phones | Three independent planes (plaster, exact vector mark, live HTML panel) with a clip-path opening from the logo's square | Hero > sticky gallery peak (5 chapters) > disclosures > direction-aware pan > row wipes > drawn process > window-widening feature > ledger > brief; 19 vh desktop | Validated brief that composes a WhatsApp/email hand-off, then the mark returns whole in the footer | The lime square is a viewfinder into five working mini-sites; the chosen one carries into the brief | Warm plaster, ink and lime, with five distinct concept palettes | 3200 |
| saraya-menu | Live surface (a working QR menu) | App chrome: sticky category tab strip with scrollspy, table chip, language link; no marketing bar | Surface already in a state: arched courtyard photo, Ghadames pattern band and the cafe card on three planes at three scroll speeds, live Tripoli open/closed status | Arrival > sticky strip > five category lists, each led by one full-width house item; natural flow, nothing pinned, ~2 vh to first item | The tray is the input: steppers, table, note, Send opens WhatsApp prefilled; on phones a bar rises into a sheet, on desktop a sticky side panel | The order writes itself: a live message bubble re-types only the line that changed, and it is exactly the text WhatsApp receives; the photo flies into the tray | Olive-black ground, Ghadames brick, real cafe photography | 4600 |

Gate for saraya-menu against wasma-next: differs on grammar, nav, hero, sequence and signature (5 of 6). The close is related (both hand off to WhatsApp), so it is not counted.

## What is taken

- Gallery-with-interludes grammar with a sticky operable stage.
- Logo-square-as-viewfinder signature move.
- Brief that inherits the visitor's gallery selection.
- Live surface menu with a self-writing order bubble.
